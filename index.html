Our Story

We met in Philadelphia in July 2024, at an international convention. One of us lived in the Netherlands, the other in Sicily: definitely not the easiest way to start a love story!

For one of us, it was love at first sight. For the other... let's just say it only took a year and a half to come around!

Thanks to a network of friends, a few coincidences and a little determination, he finally found a good excuse to come back to Italy and an even better reason to ask for a video call: a little advice about planning his trip. Needless to say, we didn't spend much time talking about the trip!

After that call, we decided to stop looking for reasons not to give it a try and start looking for flights to see each other instead. And so began the airports, suitcases, check-ins, connecting flights and trips across Europe.

We travelled together

31,200 km

77.9% of Earth's circumference

Together, we travelled around 31,000 km by plane, met each other's families and discovered, mile after mile, that it was all absolutely worth it.

In October 2026, on a cliff in Puglia, with the sun setting in the background, the big question was finally asked. The answer? "Finally!"

Because after all those miles, airports and countless goodbyes, we couldn't wait to begin our greatest journey yet: a lifetime together.

And so, from Philadelphia to Puglia, here we are, ready to celebrate our love and write the next chapter of our story.

This time, with no return ticket.
