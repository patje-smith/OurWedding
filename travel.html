Puglia, Italy

Location & Travel Info

Flights to Puglia

The closest airports are Brindisi (BDS), approx. 30 minutes away, and Bari (BRI), approx. 1.5 hours away from Lecce. Both airports offer excellent direct flight connections across Europe.

Stay in Lecce

Lecce offers a wide selection of boutique hotels, B&Bs, and authentic Masserias. We recommend staying near the historic center (Centro Storico) for the best experience.

Discover Lecce

Famous for its golden baroque architecture, delicious cuisine, and charming streets. Feel free to extend your stay and explore Puglia!
